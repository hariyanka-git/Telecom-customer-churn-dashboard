# 📊 Telecom Customer Churn & Revenue Loss Intelligence System

![Dashboard Preview](dashboard%20preview.png)

## 📌 Business Overview
This project delivers an executive-ready Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer churn risk, quantify monthly recurring revenue exposure, and provide actionable retention strategies for an enterprise telecom provider.

By combining custom risk segmentation with high-contrast metric visuals, the system enables business executives and retention teams to immediately identify at-risk customer accounts and take targeted action to safeguard revenue.

---

## 🎯 Key Metrics & Strategic Insights

* **Revenue at Risk:** Tracks real-time high-risk revenue exposure alongside total monthly recurring revenue.
* **Primary Churn Vulnerability:** High-risk customer churn is heavily concentrated in **Month-to-Month** contracts utilizing **Electronic Check** payment methods.
* **Strategic Recommendation:** Incentivize high-risk accounts to migrate toward automated payment options (Credit Card / Bank Transfer) and multi-year contracts to protect core recurring revenue.

---

## 🛠️ Dashboard Visual Architecture

* **Executive Header Banner:** Strategic navigation and high-level summary branding (`#1E2A38`).
* **KPI Metrics Row:** Quick-glance cards for Total Revenue, High Risk Revenue, Churn Rate %, and High Risk Customer Count.
* **Risk Categorization:** Donut and stacked column charts tracking customer distribution across High Risk, Medium Risk, and Low Risk tiers.
* **Granular Account List:** Integrated customer detail table allowing retention specialists to filter by contract type and execute direct customer outreach.
